About

I'm a researcher at Huawei Research in Zurich. I build performance models for early system design, focused on CPUs and memory. Currently, I'm most interested in optimizations that cross the usual boundaries between layers of the computing stack, and in how AI can help find them.

Much of my previous work looked at where a system's time and energy go and how to get them back. I did my Ph.D. at UPC in Barcelona with Antonio González and José-María Arnau, designing hardware for computer vision. I also visited the Edge Computing Lab at Harvard. I still collaborate with the ARCO group on ray-tracing hardware and co-supervise a Ph.D. student there.

I also review papers for the computer architecture community, including as a program committee member for MICRO 2026 and HPCA 2027.
